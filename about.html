About the university

An online institution founded in Monrovia to put software education within reach of students the traditional system leaves out.

Our story

The University of SDWSR was established on 6 May 2025 as an online university teaching frontend development. It began with a single question: why should a capable student in Liberia have to leave the country, or give up entirely, in order to learn to build software?

The university is owned by Dr. Samuel D. Wilson Sr. and is managed and operated by SDW Jr. It runs without a physical campus. Its classrooms are a GitHub organisation, a Google Classroom, a video library and a community of students who review each other's work.

From that first frontend cohort the curriculum has widened to cover software modelling, application development, databases, artificial intelligence, agentic systems, and the documentation and video craft that makes technical knowledge transferable.

What we believe

Access before prestige. Admission asks what you want to build, not which school you attended.

Work in the open. Everything a student builds lives in a public repository they own and keep.

Teach the whole path. A first web page, a deployed application, and a documented system are three stages of the same journey.

Local problems first. Capstone projects are expected to solve something real in the student's own community.

Mission

To give students in Liberia and other developing nations a rigorous, practical and affordable route into software, data and artificial intelligence — delivered online, in the open, and in a form that works on the connections they have.
