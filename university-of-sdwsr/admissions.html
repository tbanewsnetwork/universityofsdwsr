Admissions

One short application. No entrance exam, no application fee, and a reply to every applicant.

Who we admit

SDWSR admits anyone aged 16 or over who can read and write English, has access to a phone or computer with an internet connection at least twice a week, and is willing to submit work every week.

No prior coding experience is required for any foundation course. Advanced courses ask that you have completed the earlier course in the track, or can show equivalent work.

What happens after you apply

Within five working days

Admissions replies by email with a decision or a short follow-up question.

On acceptance

You receive an invitation to the GitHub organisation, a Google Classroom code, and your first week's lesson.

In week one

You complete SE-130, the short course on Git and GitHub that every student takes first.
